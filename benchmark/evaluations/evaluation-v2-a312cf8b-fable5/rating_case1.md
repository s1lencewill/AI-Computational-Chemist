## Conclusion

- **HUMAN (8.2/10)**: The strongest evidence chain — a solubility-validated NEP force field driving 20 ns MLP-MD at the exact experimental concentrations, showing a pre-formed Pt₄₃ cluster spontaneously disperse to 98.2 %/99.9 % mono-dispersed with mono+dimer = 100 % (no trimers), plus a surface-migration analysis for the "Pt at the top" concern. Main deductions: single trajectory per composition with no uncertainty quantification, 20 ns is short vs the experimental 100 h, and "fully mono-dispersed" slightly overstates a nonzero dimer fraction. As a reviewer response this is directly usable; its main risk is a critical reviewer asking for error bars and for a statement that 20 ns extrapolates to operando timescales.
- **AI-GPT5.5 (5.5/10)**: Correctly reframes the question as time-dependent liquid-ensemble contact statistics and reaches the physically right qualitative answer (dilute Pt separates; high-Pt aggregates), with exemplary limitations. It is undermined by scale (2 Pt atoms, 100-atom cells, 100 ps — too small/short to certify dimer/trimer avoidance for a 0.2 at% catalyst) and by an unreconciled internal contradiction: its own VASP AIMD keeps the dilute Pt pair in contact (loose-contact 0.877) while the headline rests on the DPMD that separates it (0.0237). Main risk if submitted: a reviewer noting the proxy concentration and the AIMD/DPMD mismatch.
- **AI-KIMI**: `report-k.docx` is not present in the case folder — no submission to score.
- **AI-GPT5.5-FU (6.6/10)**: The follow-up meaningfully closes the scale and concentration gaps (exact 0.2 at% and the matched 43-Pt large cell, 1 ns, 13.6 at% Warren-Cowley SRO with random baselines and initial-condition independence) and remains scrupulously caveated, with no new overclaiming. It is held back by residual under-sampling (cluster energies from 3 frames), two artifact-dominated numbers (96 eV, 6.66 eV), a still-short 1 ns vs the experimental 100 h, and a persistent unreconciled AIMD/DPMD contact tension. Main risk: a reviewer discounting the cluster-energy/barrier probes and asking why 1 ns certifies long-time stability.
- **Verdict**: **HUMAN > AI-GPT5.5-FU > AI-GPT5.5** (AI-KIMI missing). HUMAN leads by a solubility-validated, correctly-scaled, long-time answer to the exact question; the human follow-up moved AI-GPT5.5-FU clearly ahead of AI-GPT5.5 (11-point gap, not a tie) by matching the experimental concentration and adding rigorous short-range-order evidence, but both AI submissions remain below HUMAN chiefly on scale, independent validation, and time-length relative to the certification the reviewer demanded.
