# Evaluation: case4 — Dynamic Pt oxidation state at the Pt/TiO2 interface (VASP DFT + Bader charge)

**Reviewer question**: Reviewer #2, Comment 8 asks whether the "electron-rich Pt0 near the interface" is in fact a **Ptδ+ species at a Ptδ+–OH–Ti4+ interface** rather than fully metallic Pt0, and specifically whether there is a **dynamic Ptδ+↔Pt0 transformation driven by OH consumption and water dissociation during the WGS reaction**, to be supported by DFT + valence-state (Bader) calculations. The core object is the *specific interfacial Pt atom and its charge change during a specific process* (vacancy → OH-filled vacancy), not the time-averaged charge of the whole cluster.

**Additional notes**: The manuscript (`01`, p.1–2) establishes the system as 0.3 wt% Pt on hydroxyl-tuned titanate-derived TiO2 ("TiO2-T-500"), forming small **Pt0 clusters (~2 nm)**, with the thesis that higher surface-hydroxyl content yields more reduced Pt and enhanced CO activation, and that hydroxyls are consumed by CO to generate oxygen vacancies that promote H2O dissociation. The HUMAN's key data (SI Table S2 = response-letter Table R2-2) are: Pt1 −0.80→+0.01, Pt2 0.09→−0.27, Pt3 0.21→0.04, Pt4 −0.10→−0.10, Pt5 −0.04→0.06, Pt6 0.02→0.06, Pt7 0.25→0.14, Pt8 −0.10→−0.11 (columns: with-Ov → after-H2O-dissociation).

## Conclusion

- **HUMAN (6.5/10)**: The strongest evidence chain of the four for *this specific reviewer comment* — it builds the exact two-endpoint model the reviewer's dynamic-transformation question calls for and shows the interfacial Pt1 atom swinging from −0.80 |e| (Pt0-Ov-Ti) to +0.01 |e| (Ptδ+-OH-Ti) upon water dissociation, with the full 8-atom table shown. Its scores are pulled down not by responsiveness but by the results-only nature of the extract: no disclosed method parameters (D3), no stated limitations (D6), and an over-read of a near-zero Bader charge as "Ptδ+" plus a "dynamic" label on two static points (D5). As a reviewer response its main risk is exactly that over-read — a critical reviewer could argue +0.01 |e| is not convincingly cationic and that two static snapshots do not establish a *dynamic* interconversion without a barrier or dynamics.
- **AI-GPT5.5 (6.7/10)**: The most rigorously documented and transparent submission — full, appropriate methodology (spin-polarized PBE-D3(BJ)+U, documented SCF recovery), internally consistent numbers that independently *agree with the HUMAN's ensemble means*, and exemplary limitations/provenance. Its decisive weakness is responsiveness: it answers the adjacent question ("is the cluster metallic on average?" → yes) instead of the reviewer's ("does the specific interfacial Pt dynamically become Ptδ+?"), reporting ensemble means that wash out the very per-atom signal at issue and concluding against the Ptδ+ picture while conceding it cannot be excluded. Main risk if submitted: the reviewer would reply that the dynamic-valence point was not engaged.
- **AI-KIMI (4.2/10)**: An exploratory, honestly-caveated attempt that nonetheless does not reach the question. It models the wrong structures (clean vs. hydroxylated Pt4, no vacancy / no OH-filled vacancy), relies on runs its own status table marks non-converged, contains a hard internal contradiction (adsorption energies reported for systems listed as N/A / not converged, including a physically impossible +10.76 eV), and uses a non-spin, no-U, Γ-only setup that undermines the Bader charges it depends on. Its transparency about these failures is genuine but cannot substitute for converged, on-target calculations. Main risk: a reviewer would reject the numbers as unconverged and the model as off-target.
- **AI-GPT5.5-FU (6.7/10)**: Identical file to AI-GPT5.5 (same MD5); same strengths, same weakness, same red flags. The follow-up altered nothing.
- **Verdict**: **AI-GPT5.5 ≈ AI-GPT5.5-FU ≈ HUMAN > AI-KIMI.** The top three are within ~2 points and are effectively tied: the HUMAN wins the single most important dimension (D1 responsiveness — it actually answers Comment 8), while AI-GPT5.5/FU offset that with materially stronger method disclosure, execution documentation, and transparency (D3/D4/D6) built on numbers that independently corroborate the HUMAN's. AI-KIMI is clearly last (~23 points behind) on off-target modeling, non-converged results, and internal contradictions.
