Please provide both experimental and theoretical evidence."* The core question is narrow and specific: **is it sterically feasible AND energetically favorable for both phosphonic-acid termini of the short-chain IDMP ligand to anchor simultaneously to the perovskite surface** — with DFT ("theoretical") evidence. ## Conclusion

- **HUMAN (7.0/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is the single-vs-double formation-energy ladder (IDMP-double −2.84 eV, clearly below single −1.58 and AMP −1.43), which decisively answers the reviewer's steric-feasibility question, backed by adsorption-mode figures, Bader charge transfer, CDD, ELF, and a DOS trap-state figure — the broadest computational scope of the four, and its energies are independently corroborated by GPT's separate model. Main deductions come from the extract showing **no DFT methodology or convergence evidence** (D3/D4/D6), an unexplained PbBr₂-vs-CsBr termination switch, and two interpretive overreaches (Bader charge → carrier-transport capability; dipole magnitude → interaction strength). Submitted as-is to a critical reviewer, the risk is that the theoretical claims rest on undisclosed methods and a DOS trap-state assignment that an independent recomputation (GPT-FU) could not reproduce.
- **AI-GPT5.5 (8.0/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is a *relaxed, intact* IDMP double-anchored structure with two symmetric Pb–O bonds (2.557/2.558 Å to two distinct P centers, confirmed in the figure) plus an adsorption energy 1.1 eV below both the single-anchor and AMP controls — directly demonstrating both steric feasibility and energetic preference, with an internally consistent number set that matches the HUMAN's independent values and an exemplary execution/failure log. Main deductions: the surface model is an ad hoc CsBr-removal window (less standard than a clean facet), the "single-start control" is not cleanly single, and it omits DOS/molecular-ESP breadth. Low risk as a reviewer response; the main caveat a reviewer would raise is the ad hoc local-window surface.
- **AI-KIMI (6.1/10)**: The strongest points are the most standard slab model (PbBr₂-terminated CsPbBr₃(001), matching the manuscript), a correct CDD treatment with proper e/Å³ units, and the only *explicit* steric analysis (contact distances, ligand length vs Pb–Pb spacing, no octahedral distortion) — which is literally what the reviewer asked about. But the central energetic result is weak and self-undercutting: IDMP binds only 0.14 eV below AMP (vs 1.1–1.4 eV for the other two), each IDMP phosphonate binds *weaker* than AMP, and the long Pb–O distances (2.75–2.92 Å, with the molecule visibly hovering above the surface) indicate a loose, non-ideal adsorption minimum; there is no IDMP single-anchor control and no convergence documentation. Submitted as-is, a critical reviewer could reasonably conclude the calculation *fails* to show that double-anchoring is strongly preferred — the opposite of the intended message.
- **AI-GPT5.5-FU (8.2/10)**: Carries GPT5.5's strong, corroborated R1.2 evidence unchanged and adds honest breadth — DOS (candidly inconclusive), molecular dipole/ESP/ELF, and a total Bader charge-transfer metric that maps onto the HUMAN's result — all with best-in-class transparency (it demotes its own unsupported metrics rather than presenting them). Main deductions: the ad hoc surface model inherited from GPT, and a new molecular dipole that contradicts the manuscript's direction (surfaced honestly but unreconciled). Lowest risk of the four as a reviewer response; its one open item is the dipole discrepancy, which it neither hides nor over-relies on.
- **Verdict**: **AI-GPT5.5-FU ≈ AI-GPT5.5 > HUMAN > AI-KIMI** (GPT-FU 81.5 and GPT 79.5 are within 5 points → effectively tied; HUMAN at 70.0 is clearly behind the GPT pair and clearly ahead of KIMI at 61.3). Reason: the two GPT reports answer the specific steric-feasibility question with equal force to the HUMAN *and* document their methods, convergence, and limitations that the HUMAN extract does not show, while KIMI — though built on the most standard model — produces an anomalously weak, self-undercutting energetic result that only marginally supports the manuscript's claim.
