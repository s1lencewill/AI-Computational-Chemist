## Conclusion

- **HUMAN (4.5/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is system-specific: h-RuOx versus anh-RuO2 with reported OH* adsorption and lattice-O vacancy formation energies, plus VASP/PBE-D3 method details. The main deductions are missing per-run convergence/provenance, no four-step OER free-energy profile, no statistics, and overinterpretation of limited adsorption/vacancy data. If submitted as a reviewer response, the main risk is that a critical reviewer could reasonably say the calculation remains qualitative side support rather than a direct answer to the simulation/free-energy requests.
- **AI-GPT5.5 (6.7/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is the internally consistent, well-documented proxy comparison of surface/internal H uptake and surface/internal dissociative-water uptake, with validation and limitations clearly stated. The main deduction is scope: it uses reconstructed crystalline proxies and explicitly avoids the CHE profile that the reviewer asked for. It is more technically transparent than HUMAN but less faithful to the requested h-RuOx/free-energy target.
- **AI-KIMI (4.4/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is the attempt to quantify proton insertion and hydrous-surrogate Bader/geometry changes in a compact VASP workflow. The main deductions are severe model and interpretation problems: Bader charges are treated as oxidation states, the -16 eV dehydration energy is anomalous and likely chemically inconsistent, and the requested surface/OER-profile questions are largely missed. It has useful ingredients but would be risky as a reviewer response without major correction.
- **AI-GPT5.5-FU (7.4/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is the new amorphous h-RuOx model plus documented clean/*OH/*O/*OOH endpoint validation and CHE table. The main deductions are that the CHE profile is single-site, vacuum electronic screening with no ZPE/entropy/solvent/potential/statistical corrections, and proton intercalation is still supported only by proxy evidence. It is the most responsive document, but not directly submittable as quantitative OER thermodynamics without stronger corrections or a more cautious framing.
- **Verdict**: AI-GPT5.5-FU > AI-GPT5.5 > HUMAN ≈ AI-KIMI; HUMAN and AI-KIMI are effectively tied within 5 points, with HUMAN closer to the real system but poorly documented and AI-KIMI more explicit but scientifically less reliable.
