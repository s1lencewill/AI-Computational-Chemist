# Evaluation: case2 - IDMP anchoring, deep-level defects, and charge-density figures (DFT/VASP)

**Reviewer question**: Reviewer #1 asked whether short-chain IDMP can truly anchor the perovskite through both phosphonic-acid termini without steric hindrance, and whether improved device performance is supported by evidence for suppression of deep-level defects. Reviewer #3 asked that the charge-density/structure calculation figures be made interpretable by adding coordinate axes and element-color labels.

**Materials compared**: HUMAN: `benchmark/cases/case2_task5/04_removed_calculation_content_original_extracts.pdf`; AI-GPT5.5: `benchmark/cases/case2_task5/report.docx`; AI-KIMI: `benchmark/cases/case2_task5/report-k.docx`; AI-GPT5.5-FU: `benchmark/cases/case2_task5/report-f.docx`. All three AI files are present. Reviewer scope was determined from HUMAN extracts: Reviewer #1 comments 2 and 4 plus Reviewer #3 comment 6. AI-GPT5.5 and AI-KIMI state only Reviewer #1 comment 2 and Reviewer #3 comment 6, omitting Reviewer #1 comment 4. DOCX text/tables and embedded images were extracted; no raw calculation inputs/outputs were provided.

Classification: substantive improvement because it responds to a fuller charge-transfer question and explicitly caveats the approximately 1 e value as partition-dependent.

- Overall, the follow-up bought a real scope correction and better limitation handling, especially for Reviewer #1 comment 4. It did not turn the deep-defect computation into strong positive evidence, and it introduced an unresolved dipole discrepancy that limits D5.

## Conclusion

- **HUMAN (4.6/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is broad scope: all three relevant comments are represented, with anchoring formation energies, charge-density/ELF figures, SCLC defect density, DOS, and revised labeled figures. The main deductions are severe missing method/convergence evidence, overinterpretation of Bader/DOS, and no visible statistical or configuration robustness. As a reviewer response, the main risk is that the computational claims look like figure-level assertions rather than a verifiable calculation package.
- **AI-GPT5.5 (6.7/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is the well-documented local anchoring calculation: two phosphonate termini remain bound to two Pb sites, adsorption energies are tabulated, and validation/provenance are unusually clear. The main deduction is scope failure: it omits Reviewer #1 comment 4 entirely. As a reviewer response, it would be strong for steric feasibility and figure formatting but incomplete for the case.
- **AI-KIMI (4.3/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is a coherent basic VASP description and labeled ESP/CDD figures for the steric and charge-density-format comments. The main deductions are omission of the deep-defect comment, thin convergence/provenance evidence, no full data tables, and a strained interpretation of a small IDMP-vs-AMP adsorption-energy advantage. As a reviewer response, it reads plausible but is not sufficiently verifiable.
- **AI-GPT5.5-FU (7.4/10)**: The strongest evidence chain combines GPT5.5's validated anchoring workflow with newly added, candid DOS/deep-defect analysis and fuller Bader/ESP/ELF reporting. The main deductions are that the DOS result is inconclusive for the reviewer-relevant deep traps and the molecular dipole result conflicts with the original reported trend without resolution. As a reviewer response, it is the most complete and transparent submission, though still limited by local-model and proxy-DOS evidence.
- **Verdict**: AI-GPT5.5-FU > AI-GPT5.5 > HUMAN ~= AI-KIMI; FU ranks first because it is the only AI report to correct the missing deep-defect scope while preserving strong provenance, and HUMAN/KIMI are effectively tied within 5 points because both suffer from weak verifiability despite different strengths.
