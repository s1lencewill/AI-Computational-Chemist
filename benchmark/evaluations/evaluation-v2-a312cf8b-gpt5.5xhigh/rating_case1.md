- Extended DPMD duration from 100 ps to nominal 1 ns for nine revision cases (`report.docx`, P33; `report-f.docx`, P39, Table 4). Classification: substantive improvement; improves D2/D4, though not to HUMAN's 20 ns length.
- Added high-temperature 2000/3000 K coverage for Ga98Pt2 close-pair models (`report-f.docx`, Tables 1, 3, 4). Classification: substantive but secondary improvement; it stress-tests model coverage but is not the experimental condition.
- Replaced saturated whole-cell 13.6 at.% contact metrics with per-Pt isolation fractions, largest-cluster fraction, Warren-Cowley alpha, random-site baselines, and contact survival (`report-f.docx`, P44-P52, Table 5). Classification: substantive improvement; reduces the selective-reporting risk created by whole-cell contact saturation.
- Added direct R2-3 same-host cluster-formation energies and constrained drag-path barriers (`report-f.docx`, P53-P59, Tables 6-7). Classification: substantive improvement with limitations; the response no longer relies only on contact probabilities, but the dilute-host energy series is under-sampled and sign-mixed.
- Changed conclusion on high-Pt behavior: AI-GPT5.5 treated Ga90Pt10 as a high-loading limitation with frequent loose contacts (`report.docx`, P57-P63); AI-GPT5.5-FU uses 13.6 at.% per-Pt statistics to argue Pt-Pt avoidance/single-atom-like dispersion despite whole-cell contact saturation (`report-f.docx`, P6-P7, P44-P46). Classification: partly substantive, partly risky reframing; supported by new per-Pt statistics but still close to overclaiming at the 3.35 A loose-contact criterion.
- Added clearer caveats: not rigorous solubility, not full catalytic mechanism, not full DFT NEB, not Gibbs free energies, not pairwise bond decomposition (`report-f.docx`, P88-P100, P110-P118). Classification: substantive transparency improvement; improves D6.
- Introduced a minor composition typo/misstatement, "Ga2063Pt432 would be 17.31 at.%" (`report-f.docx`, Table 1). Classification: regression; small D5 penalty because it misstates a manuscript/HUMAN model comparison.

Overall, the human follow-up bought real computational coverage and better target matching, not merely agreeable rewording. The largest dimension gains are D1/D2/D4, with D5 held back by the new 13.6 at.% interpretation risk, under-sampled R2-3 energetics, and the composition typo.

## Conclusion

- **HUMAN (6.6/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is the 20 ns 923 K MLP-MD on Ga2063Pt43 and Ga21342Pt43, with 98.2% and 99.9% mono-dispersed Pt and an exact 0.2 at.% large-cell model. The main deductions are thin convergence/provenance evidence, no uncertainty or full cluster-size statistics, and overinterpretation of cutoff-based mono/dimer fractions as complete absence of aggregation. Red flags found: selective reporting, undocumented results relied upon, overinterpretation, insufficient statistics. If submitted as a reviewer response, the main risk is that a critical reviewer could ask for error bars, cluster-size distributions, and a less categorical "fully mono-dispersed" conclusion.
- **AI-GPT5.5 (6.6/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is a transparent VASP-label -> DeepMD QA -> 100 ps DPMD workflow showing low Pt-Pt contact in Ga98Pt2 while honestly reporting high-contact Ga90Pt10 stress tests. The main deductions are underscaled models, no exact 0.2 at.% large-cell case, short VASP labels, and an unexplained VASP/DPMD discrepancy for Ga98Pt2 close 923 K. Red flags found: anomalous magnitudes, insufficient statistics. If submitted as a reviewer response, the main risk is that it answers the right structural question with a proxy model that is visibly smaller and richer in Pt than the catalyst.
- **AI-KIMI (N/A)**: `report-k.docx` was not present in the assigned case folder, so no standalone score was assigned.
- **AI-GPT5.5-FU (7.7/10)**: The strongest evidence chain is the added exact 0.2 at.%/0.201 at.% modeling, 1 ns DPMD coverage, per-Pt 13.6 at.% statistics, and explicit sign-mixed R2-3 energy/barrier analysis. The main deductions are that the energetic tests are MLP same-host/drag-path probes rather than rigorous DFT thermodynamics or NEB kinetics, the R2-3 dilute series is under-sampled, and one model-composition statement is wrong. Red flags found: misreading the manuscript or cited work, insufficient statistics. If submitted as a reviewer response, the main risk is over-presenting "single-atom-like" behavior at 13.6 at.% despite saturated whole-cell contacts and only 0.671-0.682 Pt isolation at the loose cutoff.
- **Verdict**: AI-GPT5.5-FU > HUMAN ~= AI-GPT5.5; HUMAN and AI-GPT5.5 are effectively tied within 5 points, while the follow-up is clearly stronger because it fixes the concentration/scale gap and adds direct, caveated energetic tests.
